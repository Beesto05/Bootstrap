COMPANY SUPPORT - FAQ

1.How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping (1–2 days) is available at checkout.
2.What is your return policy?
Free returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.
3. Can I change or cancel my order?
Yes, within 1 hour of placing it. After that, the order is in fulfillment and can't be modified.
